Guangyuan Wang

Reinforcement learning, generative models, and optimization.

I am finishing a bachelor's degree in Honours Math & CS at McGill University and Mila – Québec AI Institute, where I do research on reinforcement learning and optimization theory.

In Fall 2026, I will be starting a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the California Institute of Technology. I'm part of the Caltech AI & ML Lab, the Theoretical CS Group, and the Chen Institute for Social and Decision Neuroscience. I'm grateful to be supported by the Kortschak Graduate Fellowship in the CMS Department.

My current research interests span foundations of generative models, reinforcement learning, and optimization dynamics, with applications to scientific discovery such as healthcare and cognitive neuroscience.

Specifically, I study reasoning and planning in agentic frameworks and foundation models, their representation geometry that makes downstream optimization well-conditioned, and generalizable discovery strategies under structured environments with verifiable feedback. Ultimately, I aim to develop AI systems that can interact and reason with limited supervision.

Feel free to reach out with questions or to chat about research. I am always open to new collaborations!
