Guangyuan Wang

I am finishing my double bachelor's degrees in computer science and mathematics with highest honours at McGill University and Mila – Québec Artificial Intelligence Institute, where I do research on reinforcement learning and optimization theory. There, I am very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Doina Precup and Prof. Elliot Paquette. Broadly, I aim to understand and advance the intelligence of agentic reasoning systems.

Feel free to reach me if you would like to discuss research.
